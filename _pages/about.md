## Beyond the horizon

When I was young, I used to climb the highest ridge on Apollo-Theta and watch the ships disappear into the sky. I wondered where they were going, what they would find, and whether I might follow them someday.

My name is Benny Starbuck. I spent most of my life preparing for that someday: learning to mend broken machines, taking salvage jobs, and saving what I could for a journey beyond the horizon. Then I found Plowshare in a collapsed depot. Its war markings had been scraped away, and its Ion Core had fallen silent. I carried its pieces home and gave it a second life.

Plowshare still bears the shape of a soldier, but I built it to be a Shepherd. It knows how to guide a frightened creature across a stream and how to stand between danger and someone who cannot face it alone. I hope we’ll find more chances to do that than to draw its sword.

Now the last repair is finished. The supplies are packed. For the first time, the horizon is a place we can go.

These pages are our captain’s log: a record of distant worlds, unexpected friends, and the questions we bring home with us. I don’t know what waits out there, or what Plowshare might remember of its first life. I only know we’re ready to find out.

If you’ve ever looked toward a distant light and felt it calling you, come along.
